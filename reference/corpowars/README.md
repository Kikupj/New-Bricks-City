# Corpo Wars — Prototype 0.1 (Guard & Stealth test)

See CHANGELOG.md for version history.

Run `Play_StealthTest.bat` in the Corpo Wars folder (or: `uzdoom -iwad freedoom2.wad -file CorpoWars +map CWTEST`).

## Controls / mechanics
- Stay out of light and crouch to lower VISIBILITY (bottom-left meter).
- Guards: UNAWARE -> SUSPICIOUS (walk over, look around) -> ALERT (combat + UEAI search).
- Guards see in a ~100 deg cone (140 deg once spooked). Distance + light + crouch + speed all matter.
- Noise: gunfire (pistol ~1000u, others ~1400u), running (~360u), walking (~160u). Crouch-walking is near silent.
- USE behind an unaware/suspicious guard = silent non-lethal takedown.
- Guards who find a downed guard raise an alarm to everyone nearby.
- Shooting an unaware guard does double damage.
- Test map: corridor switches kill/restore the main hall lights. Grab the blue key in the office.

## Mapping
- Regular guards randomly use male/female militia sprites in charcoal/navy uniforms.
  Guards and supervisors choose an original, tan, brown, or deep-brown skin tone when spawned.
  The same tone carries through their animations; palettes are defined in `trnslate.txt`.
- 20001 CW_Guard — arg0 = TID of first patrol point (0 = stationary post, faces its placed angle)
- 20002 CW_PatrolPoint — arg0 = next point TID, arg1 = wait tics (35 = 1s)
- 20004 CW_SecuritySupervisor — bald suited guard, same patrol args, 25% longer sight range.
  G01/G02 include one supervisor as the first successfully placed guard.

## Prototype 0.2 — Contract loop
Run `Play_CorpoWars.bat` (starts in HUB01).
- HUB01 (Meridian office): walk to the computer console on the north wall and press USE to open the Acquisitions Terminal (contract list, brief, payouts, DEPLOY, debriefs). Mouse or UP/DOWN + ENTER.
- M01 (Oriole Helix Lab): objectives — prototype in the office (USE), research server by the office door (shoot, loud), Dr. Vance at the east end of the dark strip (USE).
- Extract by returning to the insertion point once a primary is done. USE there to abort (costs lost).
- Payout: best primary + bonuses, +25% never spotted, -10% per alarm (max -50%), +15% no kills.
- New thing numbers: 20010 terminal hitbox (place in front of a console wall), 20020 prototype, 20021 server, 20022 scientist, 20023 extraction.
- Contracts are defined in zscript/corpowars/contracts.zs (CW_Contract.Get).

## Prototype 0.3 — Intel & operatives
Terminal tabs (LEFT/RIGHT): CONTRACTS | INTEL | OPERATIVES. Loadout is bought with the op and cleared after it.
- Intel: Site survey ($300, automap + objectives on radar), Patrol schedule ($400, guards on radar).
- Operatives (max 2): Nyx hacker ($800, B = 20s facility blackout, 1 use), Marrow cleaner ($600, downed guards vanish),
  Vox distraction ($400, N = noise lure at aim point, 3 uses). Rebind in Options > Controls > Corpo Wars.
- Ceiling lights (20115) now spawn real dynamic lights: they raise your visibility and go dark in a blackout.

## Prototype 0.4 — Rules of engagement
- Contracts can fine you: unauthorized objectives (e.g. sabotage on Brain Drain), per guard killed, per civilian killed.
  Shown in the terminal under RULES OF ENGAGEMENT; applied in the debrief even if you abort.
- 20024 CW_Civilian: Oriole staff. They witness you (lit, in their view, <600u) -> scream + alarm nearby guards, then flee.
  USE from behind = knockout (no fine). Guards find civilian bodies too. Marrow cleans them up.
- Killing Dr. Vance fails the RECRUIT objective and counts as a civilian casualty.
- CW_Contract.protectCivilians toggles the civilian rule per contract (Helix Recovery: off, Brain Drain: on).
- Debrief is sectioned: EARNINGS / RULES OF ENGAGEMENT | FIELD REPORT / ACCOUNT (full money trail).

## Prototype 0.5 — Quartermaster (gear locker)
HUB01 workbench under the GEAR wall (20012 CW_GearLocker). Budget buys:
- Suppressed pistol $1200 (slot 2, ~220u noise), Tranquilizer pistol $1500 (slot 3, darts KO in 2s, 6/op), Body armor $600 (armor each op).
- Noisemaker $150 (max 3, G), Flashbang $250 (max 2, H: 5s stun on guards with LOS, 420u).
Weapons/armor are permanent and issued at mission start; gadgets are consumed.

## Prototype 0.6 — Grid inventory (Locker / Transport / Personal)
- Quartermaster (office workbench): LOADOUT tab = drag items between LOCKER (list), TRANSPORT (6x4) and PERSONAL KIT (6x3);
  R or right-click rotates while dragging, mouse wheel scrolls the locker. SHOP tab buys into the locker.
- Only the PERSONAL KIT is issued on deploy. Ammo/dart packs load when carried. Gadgets/medkit are spent from the kit (G/H/J).
- Crew kits (Nyx rig, Marrow cleanup kit, Vox lure kit) must ride in the TRANSPORT or you can't deploy with that operative.
- In the field: the equipment crate at the insertion point (20025 CW_TransportAccess) opens TRANSPORT <-> PERSONAL.

## Prototype 0.7 — The city layer
The job board is now the city. Every job is a step in an operation run by one of 6 corps or 4 minor players, and
every job takes a day (CLOSE DAY [C] on the terminal skips one). Terminal tabs: OPERATIONS (the board), INTEL, CREW,
CITY (factions, subsidiaries, relations; BUY INTEL [I]), MARKET (auctions), LEDGER (standing, patronage [P]/[D], the
war), NEWS. Roster data: `cwcity.txt`. Design: Design Docs/city-layer-design.md; notes on what's built and how to tune
it: Design Docs/city-layer-implementation.md.

## Prototype 0.8 — Site kits
Jobs now happen where the city says they do: street blocks (K01), apartment floors and rooftops (K02), industrial
lots and docks (K03), tunnels, stations, garages and bunkers (K04), civic halls, universities and hospitals (K05),
data centres and labs (K06). Offices and HQs stay on the office floor generator. Each kit has several variants and
builds a fresh layout from the job's seed. Try one directly: `uzdoom -file CorpoWars +map K03 +cw_kitvariant 1`
(variant -1 = pick from the seed; `cw_procseed` sets the seed). `cw_kitsites 0` sends every job back to the office
floor. Design and how to add a kit or variant: Design Docs/site-kits.md.

## Prototype 0.9 — Obsidian sites
Industrial sites (warehouses, docks, plants, yards, construction sites) are now built by the Obsidian level generator
with a Corpo Wars addon. Start the game with `Play_CorpoWars.bat`: the launcher keeps a pool of Obsidian maps stocked
(the first start builds one, about a minute) and builds a fresh pool in the background for next time. Try a pool map
directly: `uzdoom -file CorpoWars CorpoWars_pool\cw_pool_industrial_CWI.pk3 +map CWI03`. Details and tuning:
Design Docs/obsidian-maps.md.
