# Income and economy: the player's money (v2, 2026-09-26)

This doc covers **your** money. The corps' economy (subsidiary yields, upkeep, the property market) is in subsidiaries-draft.md and factions-draft.md.

## Built
- **Contract payouts:** best primary objective + bonuses (ghost +25%, no kills +15%), alarm penalty (−10% per alarm, max −50%), rules-of-engagement fines, and op and loadout costs.
- **Board advance (safety net):** if you can't afford the cheapest contract, Meridian advances enough for it plus $800 of kit. Half of every later payout repays it. Shown in the office HUD and the debrief.
  - In the city design, this is a **Meridian loan**. Defaulting with no credit left = campaign loss.

## Planned: income sources
| Source | How it works | Tied to |
|---|---|---|
| **Contracts** | Generated by the city sim; pay = client wealth × target value × urgency | city-layer-design.md |
| **Side gigs** | 0–2 per day from minor players (Rustline, Local 9, NullSet, Tenants). Cheap, low risk, single floor, low pay. The grind option when money is tight. | campaign-and-city-population.md |
| **Fencing loot** | Pick up valuables on missions (laptops, drives, cash in drawers, prototypes left behind); they take grid-inventory space. Sold through **Grey Harbor Salvage** (Halvorsen). Price depends on your tier with Halvorsen; hostile = can't fence. | corp services |
| **Selling intel** | Documents found on missions sell to **Candor Analytics** (Sable) or **NullSet**, or reveal shells, treasuries and executives for your own use (fog of war). | sim-behavior-draft.md |
| **Gear buy-back** | The quartermaster buys gear back at 50%. | existing quartermaster |
| **Patron bonus** | Pledged: your patron's contracts pay more; its service is discounted. | patron system |
| **Your own subsidiaries** | Once you own subsidiaries: their net income (yield − upkeep − debt) and their niche perk for free. | own-corp path |

Retainers (a planned idea) are replaced by the **patron pledge**.

## Costs
- Op cost per contract, loadout, intel purchases, crew, corp services (priced by relation tier).
- **Office tiers:** rent for your current tier; moving up costs money and rep.
- **Meridian debt:** half of each payout while you owe. Later: interest, loans taken on purpose (to buy subsidiaries).
- **Own corp:** security, power and repair upkeep for your buildings.

## First build suggestion
Fence (via Grey Harbor) + gear buy-back + side gigs. All three reuse existing systems: items, the inventory grid, the quartermaster and the contract struct.
