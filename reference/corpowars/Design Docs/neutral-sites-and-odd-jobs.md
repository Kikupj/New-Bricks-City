# Neutral Sites & Odd Jobs (v2, 2026-09-27)

Planning only. Companion to contract-hierarchy.md, city-roster.md and building-types.md.

## Decisions
- **Neutral sites** are generated with a **fresh seed for every job**. They are never owned, traded or counted toward the win.
- **Odd jobs take a full day**, like contracts.
- **Every odd job belongs to an operation** (corp, minor player or your own corp) and plays one role in it.
- Minor-player **strongholds** are neutral sites held by a minor player; they keep a fixed seed while held.

## Two kinds of mission sites
| | **Corp properties** (67) | **Neutral sites** |
|---|---|---|
| Owned by | a subsidiary | nobody in the corp war |
| Map | fixed seed per save | fresh seed every job |
| Market / win | yes | no |
| Jobs | **contracts**: the decisive strike of an operation | **odd jobs**: preparation and support steps |

## Neutral site types
| Category | Site types |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure | **construction site**, rooftops, sewer/tunnels, parking structure, rail underpass |
| Residential | tenement, apartment block, luxury apartment (executive homes) |
| Commercial | hotel, bar/club, street market, restaurant, mall, pawn shop, car wash |
| Civic | university, hospital, transit hub, union hall, library/archive |
| Illicit | drug lab, chop shop, gambling den |

Built from the **street chunk kit** plus bigger sets (construction site, hotel, transit hub). Each job rolls district, layout, time of day, crowd size and who's guarding it. Not playable: Meridian HQ and the EarthGov garrison.

## Odd jobs

### Roles (from contract-hierarchy.md)
| Role | Examples | Effect on the operation / its contract |
|---|---|---|
| **Intel** | photograph plans, bug a meeting, tail someone | reveals security, floor, target location; lets you choose the entry |
| **Access** | steal a keycard, bribe a guard, copy a pass | a pass or a known unlocked route |
| **Weaken** | sabotage a delivery, cut a cable, pull a guard off shift | lower guard force or tech level for the strike |
| **Leverage** | find dirt, recover a document | a softer outcome (willing defection, scandal instead of sabotage) |
| **Progress** | plant evidence, move a package, pay off a witness | adds progress % directly |

### Objective templates (one primary, optional bonus)
Photograph/scan · retrieve · plant (bug, tracker, evidence, device) · meet/hand off · tail then photograph · small sabotage · intimidate/knock out non-lethally · clean up.

### Who posts them (matched to niche)
- Northgate Rail / Halvorsen Freight: construction sites, rail underpasses, parking structures
- Candor / Northstar: hotels, restaurants, luxury apartments
- Kestrel / Oriole Clinical: hospitals, tenements
- Sable Outdoor / Glasshouse Live: malls, clubs, markets
- Aegis Contracting: anywhere (clean-up, retrieval, intimidation)
- Voltaic / Corvane Distribution: construction sites, tunnels
- Helion Power: construction sites (its secret build)
- Minor players: on their turf
Shells post the shadier ones ("?" client until uncovered).

### Opposition
Site security (tech level 1–2), a minor player's crew, or hired muscle (DeePayback mob/hitman roster). Many civilians. No maglocks or passes; locked doors, lookouts, the occasional safe.

### Rewards
- Small pay, small rep with the poster.
- The operation effect of its role, plus possible intel (documents, shell links, schedules) and **leads** that surface new operations.
- Heat is tracked as usual; being identified still has consequences for the client.

## Availability
- Odd jobs appear as steps of the 6–10 running operations. With 32 subsidiaries and 4 minor players launching operations, there are usually several to choose from.
- Low or no op cost: the grind option when money is tight and the way to learn new gear.
- Operations whose link you haven't uncovered show their odd jobs with "?" for the operation.

## Build first
**Construction site** (reuses scaffolding and concrete pieces that the industrial sites need), then alley block, tenement and bar from the street kit.
