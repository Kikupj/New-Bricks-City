Industrial site maps — generated by Obsidian (obsidian-level-maker, GPL v2+, based on OBLIGE by Andrew Apted) with the
  Corpo Wars addon. They contain geometry from Obsidian's prefab library: the Oblige prefabs by Andrew Apted (CC0) and

Tactical lean (zscript/corpowars/lean.zs) — reworked from "Tactical Lean" (lean.pk3, author uncredited in the pk3):
  camera-only peek with wall tracing and guard sight of the peeking head are Corpo Wars changes.
Ledge climbing (zscript/corpowars/player.zs) — from "Ledge Climbing" by Dodopod (2018, "use or modify, attribute me");
  climbing onto props, the no-weapon crash fix and the crouched arrival are Corpo Wars changes.
Grounded movement (zscript/corpowars/player.zs) — adapted from "Build Movement" v2 by Pato Fanatico (buildmovev2.pk3),
  itself after Nash Muhandes' NashMove, with code from Can't Sleep ("Nobody Told Me About id") and BROS_ETT_311
  (UniversalPlayerMove). Slower tuning, per-player state and the prop/climb/lean integration are Corpo Wars changes.
